## Example

### `YYYY-MM-DD — Product family uses master symbol + descriptors`

- **Status:** approved
- **Scope:** product family
- **Decision:** Related products share the master brand mark and use controlled modifiers rather than independent logos.
- **Why:** Preserve shared recognition while allowing product differentiation.
- **Evidence / rationale:** Product architecture and user recognition require a branded-house relationship.
- **Alternatives considered:** Independent product marks; color-only differentiation.
- **Tradeoffs:** Requires stronger naming/modifier discipline.
- **Affected assets/tokens:** Product lockups, app icons, product accent rules.
- **Approved by:**
- **Version introduced:** v1.0
- **Follow-up required:** Validate all product icons side by side at small size.
